Research Interests

My main research lies in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Evolutionary Computation.

  • Evolutionary computation, differential evolution, particle swarm optimisation, surrogate-assisted evolutionary algorithms
  • Evolutionary machine learning, ensemble learning and transfer learning
  • Evolutionary multi-objective optimisation
  • Applications of Evolutionary Computation, including geophysics inversion, aerodynamic optimization, and others
